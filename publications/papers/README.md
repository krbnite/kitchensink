# Papers

PDFs imported from the old `krbnite.github.io/misc/papers` folder.

This is a partial historical archive, not a complete publication list. The files are kept here because they are useful evidence of older research work around space physics, dynamical systems, granular media, simulation, and visualization.

## Papers

- [2010 - Integrability Analysis of Regular and Fractional Blackmore-Samulyak-Rosato Fields](2010__Integrability-analysis-of-regular-and-fractional-Blackmore-Samulyak-Rosato-fields.pdf): 8-page PDF created from LaTeX.
- [2011 - Quiet Time Observations of the Open-Closed Boundary](2011__Quiet-time-observations-of-the-open-closed-boundary.pdf): Space Weather paper, 13 pages, DOI noted in PDF metadata as `10.1029/2011SW000688`.
- [2011 - Tapping Dynamics for a Column of Particles and Beyond](2011__Tapping-dynamics-for-a-column-of-particles-and-beyond.pdf): 17-page paper on tapping dynamics, Newtonian models, periodic taps, Hamiltonian systems, and long-wave limits.
- [2013 - Mission to the Trojan Asteroids](2013__Mission-to-the-Trojan-asteroids__Lessons-learned-during-a-JPL-Planetary-Science-Summer-School-mission-design-exercise.pdf): Planetary and Space Science paper from a JPL Planetary Science Summer School mission-design exercise.
- [2013 - Dynamical Systems Model and Discrete Element Simulations of a Tapped Granular Column](2013__Rosato__Dynamical-Systems-Model-and-Discrete-Element-Simulations-of-a-Tapped-Granular-Column.pdf): 4-page PDF connected to granular-column modeling work.
- [2014 - Analysis, Simulation, and Visualization of 1D Tapping via Reduced Dynamical Models](2014__Analysis-simulation-and-visualization-of-1D-tapping-via-reduced-dynamical-models.pdf): Physica D paper, 14 pages, DOI noted in PDF metadata as `10.1016/j.physd.2014.01.009`.
